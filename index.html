Who we are

A capital firm built around good partnerships.

Munafa Capital is a corporation focused on putting capital to work with care. We keep our approach simple: look closely, move deliberately, and stay close to the people we work with.

We're early in our story, and we'll share more about our work here as it takes shape.

What guides us

Three ideas we keep coming back to.

Look

Find the opportunity others walk past.

We take the time to understand a situation properly before we commit to it.

Build

Create value that holds up over time.

We favour steady progress over quick wins, and decisions we can stand behind.

Partner

Work alongside people we respect.

Good outcomes come from trust, clear communication and shared goals.

Our approach

How a conversation with us usually goes.

  1. First

    We listen.

    Tell us what you're working on and what you're hoping to achieve.

  2. Then

    We look closely.

    We take a careful, unhurried look at the details and ask plenty of questions.

  3. Then

    We decide together.

    If it's a fit, we agree on a clear path forward and what each side brings.

  4. After

    We follow through.

    We stay involved and keep the lines of communication open.
